AI Exposure Atlas · India · PLFS 2023-24 × NCO-2015 task statements

Exposure score β — share of the group's tasks a chat LLM can already halve in time (E1) plus half the share that needs tooling (E2)
Task labels
E0 no exposure E1 direct exposure E2 exposure with tooling
Method
  • One square is one million workers, apportioned by largest remainder: first across the three bands, then across occupation groups within a band. Bands sort their groups from most to least exposed.
  • Group scores aggregate NCO-2015 Vol II task statements with equal weights, following Eloundou et al. (2024) adapted to NCO-2015 (rubric v0.2-draft, claude-sonnet-4-6, one sample per task).
  • Groups below half a million workers in a band get no square; they are listed under the band.
